# Public Upload Checklist

Before uploading any presentation, handout, poster, figure, or template, confirm:

- [ ] No patient names, initials, dates of birth, record numbers, addresses, or exact dates appear.
- [ ] No rare clinical details could reasonably identify a patient.
- [ ] No copyrighted neuropsychological test stimuli are reproduced.
- [ ] No proprietary scoring instructions or normative tables are included.
- [ ] No EHR screenshots or internal institutional links appear.
- [ ] No unpublished collaborator data are included without permission.
- [ ] All images and figures are original, public domain, openly licensed, or used with permission.
- [ ] The file includes a citation or companion README.
- [ ] The file name is descriptive and does not contain sensitive information.
- [ ] The PDF has been reviewed after export for hidden notes or comments.
